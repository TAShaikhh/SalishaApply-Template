# Cover-letter voice sample

Replace this file with a real cover letter or detailed voice notes.

Preferred style:

- Direct and specific.
- Connect the employer's work to verified experience and projects.
- Use concrete evidence instead of generic enthusiasm.
- Keep the letter to one page.
- Do not begin with "I am writing."
- Put the candidate's full name, email, phone, LinkedIn URL, and date on
  separate lines.
